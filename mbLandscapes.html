MB Landscapes

MB Landscapes is a landscaping business that wanted a fresh, modern look to reflect their expertise and attention to detail. For this project, I created a logo, developed their brand identity, and designed a user-friendly website to showcase their services.

Project type: Branding, Web Design, Logo Design
Role: Lead Designer
Key objectives: Design a logo and brand identity that resonates with the landscaping industry, and create a website that is easy to navigate and represents the business’s services clearly and professionally.

Overview

Problem: MB Landscapes needed a brand refresh and an online presence that conveyed their expertise in landscaping while appealing to both residential and commercial clients.

Outcome: The result was a modern, professional brand identity with a distinctive logo and a clean, user-friendly website that highlights their services and makes it easy for potential clients to get in touch.

The Challenge

MB Landscapes had a lack of cohesive branding, which made it difficult to stand out in a competitive market. They also did not have a functioning website, meaning potential clients had to rely on word-of-mouth or external platforms to find them.

Our Goals

  • Design a logo that reflects the professionalism and quality of the landscaping services
  • Create a strong, recognisable brand identity that can be used across all marketing materials
  • Design a responsive, user-friendly website that showcases the business’s services and portfolio
  • Make it easy for clients to contact MB Landscapes and request quotes

What I Did

1. Logo and Brand Design

The first step was designing a logo that communicated MB Landscapes’ values: professionalism, reliability, and expertise. I opted for a clean, modern design with earthy tones to reflect the landscaping focus. The brand identity was extended across business cards, letterheads, and promotional materials.

2. Website Design

The website was designed to be simple and intuitive, focusing on showcasing MB Landscapes' services and portfolio. The clean layout made it easy for visitors to navigate, with clear calls to action for getting quotes and contacting the team. The site is fully responsive, ensuring it looks great on both desktop and mobile devices.

3. Development

I built the website using a CMS tool so that the client could easily update with new and exciting projects.

Outcomes

  • MB Landscapes now has a strong visual identity with a modern logo and cohesive branding
  • The website has increased online inquiries, with an easy-to-navigate design that encourages engagement
  • The brand and website have helped MB Landscapes position themselves as a professional, reliable landscaping service

Reflections

A strong brand and clear website is the key to success, for small businesses. Working on this project allowed me to see the transformation that a professional brand identity and an awesome website can bring to a business. MB Landscapes now has the tools to present themselves as the experts in their field they are, and I’m so pleased to have helped with this trnasformation.
